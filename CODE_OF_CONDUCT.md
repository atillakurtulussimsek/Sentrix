# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and help keep the community welcoming to
everyone regardless of background or experience. Harassment, discriminatory language and personal attacks are
not tolerated.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainers through GitHub (repository owner profile). Reports
are handled confidentially; maintainers may remove content or ban participants who violate these standards.
